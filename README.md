## What is "ghost labour"?

Ghost labour is when someone extracts **free work, free ideas, or free feedback** from developers while **hiding who they really are**, and dangles a **paid project** to keep you engaged.

Typical flow:

1. A profile with no verifiable identity contacts developers in a community, a forum, a job board or a DM.
2. They ask for "just a small sample", "a quick prototype", "your feedback on our idea" or "a free audit".
3. They keep coming back for **more feedback and more revisions**, with no contract, no budget and no real name.
4. They push you to **move off-platform** (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, SMS) to "enrol you in a paid project", where there is no record, no escrow and no accountability.
5. The paid project never starts, or the work is used and you are ignored.

You may also see "ghost" setups where several accounts **cross-pollinate feedback** among developers, so everyone ends up doing unpaid work for someone who stays anonymous.

> This repository documents **behaviour patterns**, not individuals. See [Rules for reports](#-rules-for-reports).

## 🚩 Red flags

| Red flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Won't share a real name, company, website or LinkedIn | You can't verify who benefits from your work |
| "Paid project soon" but no scope, budget or contract | The promise costs them nothing |
| Repeated requests for "feedback", "review", "quick fix" | Free consulting in small slices |
| Rush to move to WhatsApp / Telegram / Discord / SMS | Removes platform protections and records |
| Payment only "after you finish", or "after the client approves" | You carry all the risk |
| Asks for your code, repo access, API keys or credentials | Possible IP theft or account compromise |
| Asks you to pay a fee, "registration" or "enrolment" cost first | Classic advance-fee scam |
| Vague client ("a big investor", "a foreign client") | Nobody accountable |
| Pressure and urgency ("today only", "slot closing") | Prevents you from verifying |
| Shares links that ask you to log in or install something | Phishing or malware risk |

One red flag is a reason to slow down. Several together are a reason to stop.

## ✅ How to protect yourself

1. **Verify identity first.** Ask for a real name, company, website, business email and a video call. Cross-check on LinkedIn, the company registry and domain WHOIS.
2. **Get a written scope and contract** before any work beyond a clearly bounded, *paid* discovery step.
3. **Use a paid trial or milestone.** Never unlimited free samples. Your time is the product.
4. **Take an advance.** Even 20–50% upfront filters out most ghost contacts.
5. **Use platforms with escrow or invoicing** where you can. If you must go off-platform, keep everything in writing and invoice properly.
6. **Never share** credentials, private repos, API keys or OTPs.
7. **Don't pay to get work.** Real clients pay you.
8. **Watermark or limit demos.** Show screenshots or a sandbox, not production-ready code.
9. **Keep records.** Screenshots, chat exports, timestamps.
10. **Warn others** by reporting here.
